# PRODUCT.md

## Product Name
Lumen Workspace

## Product Purpose
Lumen Workspace is an enterprise AI operations cockpit where each company can organize teams, users, skills, and memory domains with strict governance. The product must let organizations run multi-user AI workflows while preserving isolation between user, team, and global scopes.

## Users
- Workspace Admin: Owns governance, policy, and cross-team visibility.
- Team Admin: Manages users and enabled skills in one team.
- Member: Uses assigned capabilities to work safely inside their team scope.

## Core Jobs
- Authenticate with workspace identity (`email + PIN`) and role-aware access.
- Use chat, tools, and settings without leaking memory across teams.
- Operate with clear governance signals: what is enabled, what is blocked, why.

## Non-Negotiables
- Security first: no cross-tenant data leakage.
- Deterministic permissions: role and team policies must be explicit.
- Fast operational clarity: dense but readable UI for dashboards and settings.

## Brand Tone
- Professional, technical, and trustworthy.
- No playful metaphors or decorative gimmicks.
- Short, direct microcopy focused on action and status.

## Register
product

## Anti-References
- Consumer-social visual language.
- Over-glassy dashboards with heavy blur.
- Decorative gradients that reduce information clarity.

## Success Criteria
- Governance actions are obvious and auditable.
- Users always understand current scope (user/team/global).
- Light and dark themes remain equally legible and consistent.
